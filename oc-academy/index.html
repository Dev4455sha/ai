⚡ AIRForge • Built for serious preparation

Study with a system, not a pile of tabs.

AIRForge is a single-workspace study tool built around the way JEE preparation actually happens: learn something, try problems, find what went wrong, revisit it and test it again. The AI layer observes your work and recommends next actions, while the final choice always stays with you.

Use the platform alongside your coaching, books, official resources and your own notes. It is a control room for your preparation—not a replacement for them.

🎯 Core principle

Evidence over guesswork.
Mastery signals are built from attempts, accuracy, Advanced performance, errors, revision activity and completed work rather than a single self-declared checkbox.

Student choice stays primary.
The AI can suggest a chapter, review or retry, but it does not silently change your study plan.

🧠 AI decision rules

Evidence weighted toward demonstrated performance. High accuracy alone is not enough: Advanced accuracy and recent revision increase confidence; repeated errors reduce it. New/unknown chapters remain unmastered until the system sees evidence.
