🧠 Five laws of the Academy
1. Electrons move first.Curved arrows start from electron pairs or bonds, never from the positive center.
2. Mechanism before name.Identify the actual electron movement before recognizing a named reaction.
3. Compare pathways.Olympiad problems often hide competing intermediates or stereochemical routes.
4. Explain exceptions.Don't memorize an exception without understanding why the normal rule fails.
